1. Purpose
Trio Infrastructure Private Limited recognises that an essential prerequisite for sustained productivity is taking
time off from work. The Company believes that holidays and time to unwind are important for employee well-
being. These leave guidelines are designed to support work-life balance and encourage employees to avail
themselves of their entitled leave.
2. Scope
This policy is applicable to all full-time employees of Trio Infrastructure Private Limited across all offices, project
sites, and work locations in India, unless a specific employment contract provides otherwise.
3. Definitions
For the purpose of this policy, the following definitions shall apply:
Term Definition
Working Day Any day on which the employee is required to report to work as per the applicable
weekly off rotation, excluding public holidays declared by the Company.
Leave Year 1st April to 31st March.
EOM Accrual End-of-Month accrual — leave credited at the end of each month.
LOP Loss of Pay — deduction of salary for unauthorised or excess leave beyond
entitlement.
Comp-Off Compensatory Off — a day off granted in lieu of working on a weekly off or
declared holiday.
Reporting Manager The immediate supervisor to whom the employee directly reports.
4. Types of Leave & Entitlements
The following categories of leave are available:
4.1 Annual Leave (AL)
Annual Entitlement: 18 working days per year | Accrual: End of Month (EOM)
• All confirmed employees are entitled to 18 (Eighteen) days of Annual Leave per year, which may be
availed for any purpose.
• Annual Leave shall accrue at the rate of 1.5 day per month, credited at the End of Month (EOM) upon
completion of each month of service.
• Leave is not permitted during the probation period. However, leave entitlement will continue to accrue
and will be credited upon confirmation.
• Leave will be counted on working day principle. In case a Weekly Off or Fixed Holiday gets sandwiched
between AL dates, the intermediate days will not be treated as AL.
• Leave must be applied for in advance and is subject to approval by the Reporting Manager based on
operational requirements.
• Any AL availed without prior approval or beyond available leave balance shall be treated as Leave
Without Pay (LOP)
• At the end of each year, employees may carry forward a maximum of 5 (five) unutilised Annual Leave
days to the subsequent year. Any balance in excess of 5 days shall lapse automatically on 31st March.
• Carried-forward AL must be utilised within the succeeding year

4.2 Public Holidays
• Employees are entitled to public and statutory holidays as declared by the Company annually through
the official Holiday List circulated by HR before the commencement of each calendar year.
• Holidays falling on an employee's designated weekly off day shall be treated as holidays only; no
compensatory off or substitute holiday shall be provided.
• The Company may, at its discretion, substitute a notified holiday for another working date in case of
operational necessity, with advance notice to all employees.
4.3 Compensatory Off (Comp-Off)
• An employee required to work on a designated weekly off day or on a Company-declared public holiday
shall be entitled to a Compensatory Off (Comp-Off) in lieu of such work.
4.4 Maternity Leave
• Subject to the provisions of the Maternity Benefit Act, 1961, eligible female employees shall be entitled
to:
◦ 26 (twenty-six) weeks of paid maternity leave for the first two surviving children.
◦ 12 (twelve) weeks of paid maternity leave for a third or subsequent child.
◦ 12 (twelve) weeks of paid leave for adopting or commissioning mothers.
◦ 6 (six) weeks of paid leave in case of miscarriage or medical termination of pregnancy.
• Eligibility requires a minimum of 80 (eighty) days of actual service with the Company in the 12 months
immediately preceding the expected date of delivery.
• The leave must be applied for at least 4 (four) weeks before the expected delivery date, accompanied by
a medical certificate from a registered gynaecologist or obstetrician.
• Maternity Leave is independent of and does not affect the employee's Annual Leave entitlements.
4.5 Paternity Leave
• Male employees are entitled to 5 (five) working days of paid paternity leave in recognition of their role
and responsibilities during significant family milestones.
• Paternity Leave shall be availed within 30 (thirty) days of the birth of the child.
• This benefit is available for up to 2 (two) surviving children.
• Paternity Leave is non-cumulative, non-carry-forward, and shall lapse if not availed within the prescribed
period.
• The leave must be applied in advance
5. Attendance, Working Hours & Time Compliance
5.1 Attendance Marking
• All employees are required to mark their attendance through the Company's biometric system
• Attendance must be recorded at both check-in and check-out times on each working day.
• If an employee is unable to mark attendance on a particular working day due to technical or other genuine
reasons, the attendance must be regularised with HR within the next 2 (two) working days, accompanied
by a reason and the Reporting Manager's approval.
• Failure to mark attendance may result in the day being treated as Annual Leave or LOP, as applicable.
5.2 Working Hours, Late Arrivals & Missed Punch-Ins
• The standard working hours are 9 (nine) hours per day (inclusive of a 1-hour lunch & tea break). The
standard office timings are 10:30 a.m. to 7:30 p.m. on all working days.
• We encourage flexibility in managing work schedules, while ensuring that overall working hours are
effectively fulfilled.
• Employees may avail a start time flexibility of up to 30 (thirty) minutes beyond the scheduled shift start
time on regular working days; such flexibility is expected to be appropriately balanced to ensure

completion of daily working hours. No start time flexibility or grace period shall be applicable on
Saturdays, Sundays, or Public Holidays where an employee is scheduled to work.
• A flexibility of up to 1 (one) hour in end time may also be availed, subject to corresponding adjustment in
start time and prior alignment with the reporting manager.
• Any deviation beyond the defined flexibility window will be recorded as a ‘Late Remark’ in the attendance
system.
Policy Provision: 3 (three) instances of missed punch-ins OR 3 (three) instances of late arrivals (beyond
the flexibility period) in any given month shall collectively be counted as 1 (one) day of Annual Leave.
• A 'Missed Punch-In' is defined as a failure to record either check-in or check-out on a working day without
subsequent regularisation.
• Accumulation of 3 late arrivals or 3 missed punch-ins (or a combination totalling 3) within a month shall
result in a deduction of 1 (one) Annual Leave Day from the employee's balance. In the absence of any
leave balance, the day shall be treated as LOP.
• Habitual late-coming or repeated missed punch-ins, regardless of accumulated deductions, may attract
a formal warning, and continued non-compliance may result in disciplinary action.
6. Weekly Offs & Rotational Leave

Working Days Monday - Sunday Monday - Saturday
Weekly Off Rotational Offs Sunday
• Each employee at Site shall be assigned a weekly off schedule at the time of joining, or as communicated
by Reporting Manager.
• Changes to weekly off schedules shall be communicated with a minimum of 7 (seven) days' advance
notice, except in cases of operational emergency.
• Employees are not entitled to demand fixed weekly offs on specific days and must be flexible in
accordance with project timelines and business needs.
7. Leave During Notice Period
• During the notice period (whether tendered by the employee or initiated by the Company), availing of
Annual Leave or Comp-Off — is subject to the prior approval of the Reporting Manager and HR.
• Any leave availed during the notice period without explicit approval shall result in a proportionate
extension of the notice period by an equivalent number of days, unless expressly waived by
Management.
• Employees are strongly encouraged to complete their notice period in full service and avoid availing
leave during this period to facilitate a smooth transition.
8. Policy Review & Amendments
The Company reserves the right to review, modify, amend, or withdraw any provision of this Leave and
Attendance Policy, in whole or in part, at any time without giving prior notice. In the event any changes are
made, the revised terms and conditions shall be communicated to you appropriately.
