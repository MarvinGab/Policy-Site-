1. Purpose & Scope

This Code of Conduct (“Code”) sets out the standards of behaviour and professional ethics expected of every employee, contractor, consultant, and associate of Trio Infrastructure Private Limited (“the Company”). This Code is designed to foster a culture of integrity, mutual respect, and accountability across all levels of the organisation.

This Code applies to all full-time employees, part-time staff, interns, and third-party representatives acting on behalf of the Company, across all offices, project sites, and work locations in India.

2. Professional Conduct & Integrity

Every employee is expected to conduct themselves with the highest level of professionalism, honesty, and integrity in all dealings — whether with colleagues, clients, vendors, or the public.

Act with honesty, transparency, and good faith in all professional interactions.

Fulfil work responsibilities diligently and to the best of your abilities.

Represent the Company accurately and truthfully to clients, stakeholders, and authorities.

Refrain from engaging in any dishonest, fraudulent, or deceptive practices.

Avoid actions that could embarrass, discredit, or cause reputational harm to the Company.

Maintain punctuality and adhere to working hours and attendance commitments.

3. Workplace Respect & Anti-Harassment

Trio Infrastructure is committed to providing a workplace free from discrimination, harassment, and hostility. Every individual deserves to be treated with dignity and respect.

Treat all colleagues, clients, and visitors with respect, courtesy, and fairness.

Any form of verbal, physical, or psychological harassment — including sexual harassment — is strictly prohibited.

Discrimination based on gender, religion, caste, race, nationality, disability, or marital status will not be tolerated.

Bullying, intimidation, threatening behaviour, or any form of victimisation is a serious misconduct.

All complaints of harassment will be investigated promptly and confidentially in accordance with applicable law (including the POSH Act, 2013).



The Company has constituted an Internal Committee (IC) in compliance with the Prevention of Sexual Harassment (POSH) Act, 2013. Complaints may be submitted confidentially to HR or directly to the IC.

4. Confidentiality & Data Protection

Employees have access to confidential business information as part of their role. It is each employee’s responsibility to safeguard such information.

Do not disclose any confidential or proprietary business information to unauthorised persons, inside or outside the Company, without prior written approval.

Client data, project plans, financial details, HR records, pricing, and trade secrets are confidential.

Confidentiality obligations continue even after separation from the Company.

Handle personal data of employees, clients, and stakeholders responsibly and in compliance with applicable privacy laws.

Any breach of confidentiality must be reported to management immediately.

5. Conflict of Interest

Employees must avoid situations where personal interests conflict, or appear to conflict, with the interests of the Company.

Disclose to HR or your Reporting Manager any personal, financial, or family relationship that may influence — or be perceived to influence — your professional decisions.

Do not engage in employment, directorship, consultancy, or business activities without prior written approval from the Company.

Do not accept gifts, favours, or hospitality from clients or vendors that could influence business decisions. Nominal gifts of token value (under ₹500) in line with cultural norms are generally acceptable.

All conflicts of interest, once disclosed, will be assessed and managed appropriately by Management.

6. Use of Company Assets & Technology

Company assets — including equipment, systems, office supplies, vehicles, and facilities — must be used responsibly and primarily for business purposes.

Employees are prohibited from using Company resources for personal gain, outside business, or activities that violate this Code.

IT systems, email, internet, and software must be used in accordance with the Company’s IT guidelines.

Unauthorised installation of software, sharing of login credentials, or access to restricted systems is prohibited.

Company property must be returned in good condition upon separation from the organisation.

7. Health, Safety & Environment

The safety and wellbeing of all employees — particularly those working at project sites — is of paramount importance to the Company.

Comply with all applicable health, safety, and environmental laws, regulations, and Company protocols.

Report any unsafe conditions, near-misses, accidents, or injuries to the site supervisor or HR immediately.

Do not report to work under the influence of alcohol, narcotics, or any substance that impairs judgment.

Follow all site-specific safety guidelines, PPE requirements, and emergency procedures.

Active participation in safety training and drills is mandatory.

8. Social Media & External Communication

Employees must not post, share, or publish any content that disparages the Company, its leadership, clients, or colleagues on social media or any public platform.

Do not share confidential, financial, or project-related information on personal social media accounts.

Any media enquiries, press releases, or public statements on behalf of the Company must be approved by the Director before publication.

Employees are personally responsible for content they post online — even in personal capacity — if it reflects on the Company’s reputation.

9. Compliance with Laws

All employees must conduct business in full compliance with applicable laws and regulations of India, including but not limited to labour law, taxation, anti-corruption statutes, and environmental regulations. Bribery or corruption of any kind — whether giving or receiving — is strictly prohibited and may result in immediate termination and criminal prosecution.

10. Reporting Violations & Whistleblower Protection

Employees who become aware of any violation of this Code, applicable law, or Company policy are strongly encouraged to report it promptly.

Reports may be made to your Reporting Manager, HR, or directly to the Director.

All reports will be treated with strict confidentiality.

The Company prohibits retaliation against any employee who makes a good-faith report of a suspected violation.

Deliberate false reporting, however, may itself attract disciplinary action.

11. Disciplinary Action

Violations of this Code will be subject to disciplinary action commensurate with the severity of the breach and the circumstances. Disciplinary measures may include:

Verbal or written warning.

Suspension with or without pay pending investigation.

Forfeiture of benefits, incentives, or accrued entitlements.

Termination of employment for serious or repeated violations.

Legal action where violations involve criminal conduct or significant financial loss to the Company.

12. Policy Review & Amendments

The Company reserves the right to review, modify, amend, or withdraw any provision of this Code of Conduct, in whole or in part, at any time without giving prior notice. In the event any changes are made, the revised terms and conditions shall be communicated to all employees and shall take effect from the date of communication.
